It understands transcripts and visual metadata, retrieves relevant moments, and uses Claude as the reasoning layer for editorial intent, evidence-grounded story planning, selection decisions, and quality review before human approval.

WHY CLAUDE

Claude is the reasoning layer — not the archive.

Retrieval finds candidate evidence. Claude reasons over that evidence to interpret editorial intent, build an evidence-grounded story plan, explain selection decisions, and apply quality-review criteria before human approval.

Brief → Intent

Interpret audience, format, tone and editorial objective.

Evidence → Narrative

Connect retrieved moments without losing source context.

Context → Decisions

Explain selection, ordering and continuity choices.

Draft → Review

Apply review criteria before a human approves the output.

BUILT FOR RESPONSIBLE MEDIA WORKFLOWS

Source provenance

Authorized media

Human approval
